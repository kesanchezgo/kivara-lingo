## Results summary

All tested Standard/VIP cards passed the quality assertions in the probe.

### Strong outcomes

- `give` VIP ranks `dar` first and preserves alternates: `obsequiar`, `regalar`, `prestar`, `conceder`, etc.
- `wonderful` VIP avoids noun-like `maravilla` as primary and ranks adjective glosses.
- `anything` and `anybody` rank `algo` / `alguien` first in positive questions while preserving `nada` / `nadie` as alternate meanings.
- `know` ranks `saber` first for `I don't know` and preserves `conocer`.
- `break up`, `piece of cake`, and `make a decision` retain safe offline/MWE meanings and do not get overwritten by unsafe VIP rows.
- VIP collocations now prefer higher-quality phrase sources over noisy Datamuse n-grams.

### Known remaining quality work

These are not blockers, but they are the next quality improvements:

1. Some collocations are still broad or source-specific, especially `run`, `know`, `anybody`, and `anything`.
2. `run` preserves many valid alternate meanings, but the list should eventually be grouped by sense.
3. `week` VIP includes useful alternate phrases, but `semana` must remain primary.
4. `break up` VIP keeps several physical-split alternates after the relationship meaning. This is acceptable for learner breadth, but future sense grouping would improve readability.
5. The probe is intentionally not committed as a normal test because it is network-dependent. The generated report is committed instead.

## Policy confirmed


Context changes order, not coverage. Clean alternate meanings are preserved.
